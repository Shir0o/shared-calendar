# Shared Calendar

A shared team calendar for browsing, creating, and importing events. It detects time conflicts between events; it does not model attendees or personal availability.

## Language

**Conflict**:
Two timed events whose time intervals overlap. Conflicts are event-vs-event and ignore all-day events, deadlines, and holidays.
Product copy prefers "overlap" (_e.g._, "Overlaps with 2 events").
_Avoid_: Double-booking, clash
